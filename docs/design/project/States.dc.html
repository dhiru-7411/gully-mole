Player states

One color and one icon per state, as the GDD asks. Shown with style A; any style gets the same overlays. The spy must look exactly like the team they sit in, so nothing marks them except the iced hand.

Normal

Free to move, tag and carry.

Carrying the object

Gold ring + the potli (loot pouch).

Frozen

Light blue ice. Identical whether an enemy or the spy did it.

Shame freeze

1 rescue left

Wrong arrest. Drag to a fireplace.

Out

Cracked grey ice. Caught spy or 2nd wrong arrest. Stays till round ends.

Leader

Fire lamp. Everyone can see who leads.

Detective

Badge seen only by own team (incl. the spy inside it).

Our mole

Chor see this on their spy in Police. Police see a normal teammate.

Iced hand

The spy's clue. Only nearby players see it. 20 s or until a fireplace.

Footprints

Left by a secret freeze. No name, fade over 20 s.

Protected

Leader just thawed: can't be frozen for 2–3 s.

Disconnected

Faded, can't move. Can still be frozen or robbed.
